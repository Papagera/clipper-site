Clipper

Clipper is a personal, single-user tool. It helps its owner manage short videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does:

  • uploads short videos that the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own channel;
  • reads view, like and comment counts for the owner's own videos;
  • reads YouTube Analytics (watch time, audience retention) for the owner's own channel.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It does not access any other person's data.
