Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who uses this app

Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos approved by the owner to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to read the owner's channel ID and statistics of the owner's own videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read analytics (watch time, retention) of the owner's own channel.

The app does not access data of any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by this app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. It does not use the data for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the owner deletes the locally stored token.
